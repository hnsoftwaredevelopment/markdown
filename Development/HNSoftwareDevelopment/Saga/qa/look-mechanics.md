# Atelier-Salamander look mechanics

The salamander keeps its feet and lower belly anchored on a stable baseline. Its large amber eyes and eyelids lead attention; the rounded head and upper torso follow with a small turn or bend, while the tail follows one beat behind. Goggles remain securely seated on the forehead and the brass hip charm follows the pelvis without switching sides.

- 000 up: pupils and eyelids lift, chin and snout tip rise slightly; belly and feet remain planted.
- 090 screen-right: snout, nearer eye, and head turn toward the right edge; tail curves faintly left as follow-through.
- 180 down: eyelids lower and snout dips toward the belly; upper body compresses subtly without whole-body rotation.
- 270 screen-left: snout, nearer eye, and head turn toward the left edge; tail curves faintly right as follow-through.

Diagonals make even 22.5-degree changes between those cardinal families. Preserve eye construction, scale, silhouette, baseline, palette, markings, goggles, and hip charm. No whole-sprite rotation, shadows, labels, or detached effects.
