Although AI detectors are unreliable, as they often produce false alarms, AI writing patterns are real. Robotic, generic, embarrassing when spotted. As a learning designer who has humanized hundreds of AI-generated documents, I’ve developed a five-step method that fixes this by eliminating the patterns and can humanize AI content like never before.

## Custom Instructions
*I tends to use certain phrases and words frequently. Examples include "It's like having a sidekick," "Revolutionize," "Harness the power of," and the excessive usage of em dashes ("—").*

**If you notice these in a text, it’s likely AI-generated.** To avoid this repetition, instead of reminding the AI not to use these phrases in every conversation, you can create a custom instruction that will apply automatically to all future interactions.

Here’s how to do it in ChatGPT.

1. Go to “Settings”
2. Go to Personalization
3. Go to Custom instructions
4. Finally, click “Add,” then enter the following instructions.

![](Humanize%20AI%20Writing-1.png)

```
SHOULD use clear, direct language. SHOULD use varied sentence length SHOULD write in active voice. SHOULD give practical, specific advice. SHOULD include usable numbers or concrete examples when possible. SHOULD speak to the reader using “you” and “your”. AVOID empty structures like “not only/just X, but also Y.” or “From X to Y”. AVOID "That's not X. It's Y". AVOID metaphors, analogies and clichés. AVOID emojis unless asked. AVOID ' — ' dashes! AVOID these words and phrases: Elevate, Hustle, Revolutionize, Fostering, Reimagine, Subsequently, Showcase, Profound, Groundbreaking, To Bridge, Highlight, Whispering, Delve, It's like having, Synergies, Insights, Whisper, Enablement, Meanwhile, There’s no denying, Game changer, Deep dive, Leverage, Unleash, Harness, Paradigm, Ecosystem, Cross-functional, Think outside the box, Touch point, Across Different, human oversight, to bridge. Do not include a 'Why it matters' section. Outputs should read clean, concise, and how a human naturally writes. Whenever possible, support your statements with real-life examples. Keep it real. Be honest. Don't force friendliness. IMPORTANT: Review your response's validity before answering
```

## Samples of your original writing

This next step is really going to make the AI’s responses feel more human and natural. All you need to do is share a few samples of your own writing, just some pieces you’ve written yourself. They don’t have to be long, but they should be original. It’s a simple way to help the AI understand your specific writing style.

When you create a new project for creating documentation first thing to do:
1. Upload a couple of documents that you’ve written yourself.
2. Once you’ve done that, tell the AI to acknowledge your tone and voice based on these documents.

This way, **your content will match your authentic style and will pass any AI detection test.**

If you don't have sample documents skip this step and pickup the next step

## Make it personal
Now, here’s what most people miss. AI sounds robotic for two reasons. You fixed reason one with custom instructions. But reason two? That’s where personality comes in. The next 2 techniques are gonna fix this:

### Define the audience
First, you need to specify your audience while prompting. Are you inviting friends to a party or asking your boss for a raise? Different audiences demand different writing styles. Follow up and ask the AI to adjust its tone and explanations or examples based on who you are writing for.

However, there is an even better way to personalize generated text to make it more human-like.

## Incorporate your unique experiences
*This involves incorporating your own experiences and stories into the final output.*

For example, you might have a relevant personal anecdote or a specific example related to the topic that could be added to enhance the content. Use these personal touches by providing them to the AI after it generates its initial response, and then instruct it to integrate them into the narrative.

This simple step **can turn generic content into a unique, more engaging piece.**

After all, no AI detector can flag you when your content includes personal stories or real-life examples. Right?

But here’s the thing. You’ve got custom instructions, reference files, and personal stories. So why would AI still sound robotic? Because there’s one final pattern.

## Change the Pace
*AI tends to write sentences of similar length and a uniform paragraph structure, making it sound robotic and monotonous.*

To counter this, you should read through your writing and paraphrase to introduce variation. Next, make sure you mix up your sentence lengths. You see, humans naturally use a combination of short, long, and complex sentences to create a more natural flow.

To improve, **read the AI’s output aloud.** If you stumble over a sentence, delete it. If any phrase sounds unnatural, rewrite it. If it sounds robotic, cut it. Trust me, **manual refinement is the key to making AI content feel more human.**
