# How to use the Traktatielijst of Beam team 1

## What is Traktatielijst??
Every two weeks, except during holiday periods when Steely's is closed, someone from BT1 provides a treat.
The traktatielijst (treat list) allows everyone in BT1 to indicate which snack they would like, as well as whose turn it is to provide the treats.

## Goal
Understand how tu use the traktatielijst, how you specify your snack, but also how available snacks,, team members and vacation and treat data.

## Prerequisites
- To be able to use alle functionality of the Traktatielijst it has to be opened in the Excel desktop application, not the web or Teams version of Excel.
- Do **==not==** edit the content on the tab page: Invullijst, this is automatically filled
- Do **==not==** edit the content on the tab page: Rooster YYY, this is automatically filled

## Automatic refresh
It is important to know the list will automatically reset when entered before Tuesday morning (0:00), or after Wednesday noon (12:00). unless you turn off automatic refresh.
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-1.png) 
Do not forget to turn it back on after the treat moment so the list wil be emptied again for the next treat.

## Who's turn is it
You can see, in the Tab "Invullijst" who's turn it is for the treat this week, its shown on the left and the bottom of the sheet. It can also show "Niemand trakteert deze week" (Nobody wil do a treat this week, or "Vakantie periode" Vacation period, Steely';'s is closed.

If you want to see the treat-schedule for this year go to the tab "Rooster" here you can see who does the treat for a specific week (marked with an 🍽️) , or when Steely's is closed (marked red) .
> Do not change the content of this sheet manually!

## Fill in the snacklist
Ik you want to fill the treat list, just select the preferred snack from the pull down after your name, also when you ar not there or want nothing in that case select  "Afwezig/niets"
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-2.png)
## It's my turn, how can I use the list
When its your turn to do the treat just open the snacklist Tuesday afternoon @ 15:00
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-3.png)
You see in the example 1 person did not fill in the list, searching for "Kies snack" you see Marcel dit not filled in the list yet, just ask him to do it before you order.

You can see the total amount you ave to pay the next day.

At the right you see the list with totals, this list you can use to fill in Steely's order form, fill in the form for the next day 9:30

## There is a new snack at Steely's
When there is a new snack at Steely's you can add that to the list verry easy, for now we use the max cost per snack of € 1,25.
Go to the tab "Datasheet"
At the left you see the list with snacks, just add the new snack at the bottom the bottom of the list
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-4.png)
> Attention: 
> "Kies snack" should also be the first one, with no costs
> "Afwezig/niets" should be in the list with no cost


## We have a new team member
New team members can always be added to the list, but if you look at the Tab "Datasheet" you will find 3 columns with the names of the team member, witch column is used for what and do you have to enter a name in all columns? fin the answers right here.

### Show name on the "Invullijst"
When you want the name of the team member on the "Invullijst", what will be always the case if a new team member wants to join, use the first column with names
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-5.png)

Just enter the name at the bottom of the list and the name will be on the "Invullijst"

### The new team member also has to organize a treat moment
In fact the second column with names is for that purpose, but, most of the time the schedule for the entire year is already filled in, but you can always decide to replace one of the names in it wih the new team member.
First column is the persons name, second column is the week of the treat preceded with "Wk ", So "Wk 31" means person does a treat in week 31.
If the entered week number is within a range Steely's is closed the week number will turn red and you have to select another week.
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-6.png)

### Default order of treating
As a preparation for next years list there is also al list with the default order to treat, just enter the new members name at the bottom, how this list is used will be described later
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-7.png)


## I want to do  treat at an extra moment
Feel free to do that, Just use the "Traktatievolgorde" part of the Datasheet tab. enter your name at the bottom of the list and next to your name enter the week number preceded with "Wk " If you want to do a treat on another day then Wednesday make sure you turn of the automatic clearance function.

if you do an extra treat, the same week as another teammember does a treat, no problem, the first teammember that is scheduled for a treat that week will be mentioned in the Invullijst.

So If Jan and Klaas want to do a treat in Wk 31, Jan Wednesday and Klaas Friday, both can use the sheet, but only Jan's name will appear on the Invullijst

## Steely's closed
In the Datasheet we also keep the vacation periods, where Steely's is closed.
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-8.png)

These weeks will be marked red in the Tread schedule and also shown as vacation on the fill list.
If there is an additional week they are closed just enter the week number preceded with "Wk ", So "Wk 28" means Closed at leased on Wednesday of week 28, just enter it at the bottom of the list, the week numbers do not have to be in a specific order.
### I want to treat on a snack during Steely's vacation
Feel free to remove the week number from the list, no worries that where will be a gap in te list. Do **==not==** delete the line! because there is more data on that line then just the vacation week.

Be aware that if you want to do a treat you will need specific instructions for the team so they know what snack is available, but that is treaters responsibility.

## We have a new snack year
We have to do 2 things for a new snack year:
1. Make a new treat order list
2. Enter Steely's known vacation periods

### Make a new treat order list
To be sure the treats are equaly divided over the years we maintain a default treat order.
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-7.png)

When Frank did the last treat of the past year we wil first select from Marcel til the end of the list and copy this part to the top of the treat order list
![](Traktatielijst.xlsm-7.png)

Then we select the entire list from top to bottom and copy it for the other weeks in the treat order list.

Be sure the extra treat moments from the previous year are removed, and the week numbers are correct
