Waarom is je aanwezigheid van groot belang?

 

Zie hieronder een samenvatting van de resultaten van Our Enterprise survey 2018, waarbij de scores naar onder gingen.

 

- **Collaboration/ Involvement/Manager Support/ Workload**

- Findings

- - Managers don’t communicate/ share enough the information towards their team + the employees say that managers don’t show interest in their development
  - Lack of involving employees: they get too little empowerment, are not stimulated to think along & focus on continuous improvement
  - Lack of collaboration between their own teams and cross team, do not share enough information with each other or other departments.
  - Employees indicate to experience a too high workload

- **Agility & Risk Tolerance**

- Findings

- - New ways of working are not adopted quick enough. Not enough anticipation and planning for future changes
  - Employees indicate that there is no risk tolerance when it comes to trying out new things to improve business

- **Development & Opportunity**

- Findings

- - Employees share that they are not supported by WK to develop new skills and they indicate they do not receive enough feedback on their performance, in order to strengthen possible improvement areas 

 

Ongetwijfeld als leider binnen TAA NL hecht je belang aan het creëren van een positieve werkomgeving, het stimuleren van open communicatie en een vlotte samenwerking.
