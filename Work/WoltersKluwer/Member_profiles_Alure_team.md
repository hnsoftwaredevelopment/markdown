## What to expect

* Large legacy codebase
* Maintenance  and new development in the existing application
* An on-premise application used by accounting firms
* Web portal for communication between an accountant and his customers
* Services and API's for connectivity to third-party products



## What we expect

* Dare to discuss with PO about functionality to build or repair
* Dare to discuss with tech lead about the technical solution
* Dare to discuss improvements, to get them on the backlog or in the sprint



## What we need

4 (Medior/Senior) Developers, at least 2 Seniors with experience on building on-premise applications and at least 1 Senior/Medior with experience in building and maintaining web applications

1 Tester, that can write and maintain automated tests in C#, and other tools, but also is able to test manually
