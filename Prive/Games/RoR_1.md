# Roads of Rome Walkthrough & Cheats


  

## Hints


  

**General Strategy**

1. 
  A good general build strategy is to do the following:
 2. Upgrade the camp to level 2.
 3. Build the following first if you can get to them.  Sawmill and farm.
 4. Usually build the mine next because you can't clear obstructions without the mine.
 5. Build the quarry.
 6. Next if you have storehouses, you should build those even if you have to work to get to
    them.  These add 1 resource to each load brought in, including berries, trees, gold piles, and stone piles.
 7. Upgrade the storehouse if you have an upgrade available.
 8. Then start upgrading your buildings.  Most of the time you don't need to upgrade the sawmill. 
 9. Consider upgrading your camp before upgrading other buildings because of the worker plus speed benefit. 
 10. Don't start building the road until you know you have enough food to remove the obstacles to all of the production buildings including the storehouse

**In addition:** 

1. Try to keep your workers moving as much as possible.

  2.  Always pick the berries if your worker is not doing anything else.

  3.  Prioritize picking berries on all levels except the snowy ones because they
      regenerate. Snow levels don't regenerate their berry bushes.

  4.  Pick up resources at the building quickly because they don't start producing again
      until the resources are picked up.

  5.  The further apart things are, the more you need a higher level camp.  Seriously
      consider upgrading your camp to at least level 3 before upgrading other buildings unless you are seriously short of a particular resource.

  6.  Don't feel bad about chopping down trees.  We're Romans; we're not conservationists.  Seriously you will need a lot of wood to get things going quickly.

  7.  Don't feel bad about using up your food to chop down trees to get your buildings producing quickly.  Just keep an eye on whether you have obstructions blocking your
      buildings that you need the food for.

  8.  If you aren't successful the first time, switch things up a bit.  Notice what you were
      running short of and prioritize that the next time.  Seriously consider upgrading your warehouse sooner than you might think it needs to be done.

  9.  Don't start building your road until you know you have enough food to get through the rocks blocking the way to the storehouse. 

**Gameplay Information**

Episodes and levels:  Level numbering is a bit confusing.  On the map, it gives you episodes and levels.  On the introduction page, it gives you level numbers for the whole game.  So episode 1 level 5 is actually level 5.  Each episode, 4 in all, is in a different terrain.  While buildings, roads, bridges, and resources do not cost anymore in later levels, all the obstacles cost increasingly more.  Also berry bushes regenerate slower in later levels.

Timer:  Clicking anywhere on the screen start starts the timer.  You cannot check costs until the timer has started, so just click, check things out, and then restart your level after you have decided your plan of attack.  If you finish before the little line on the left, you will receive an expert rank.

Workers:  With each level you start with 1 worker.  Each time you upgrade the camp you get an additional worker and all of them move and work more quickly.  So sometimes even if you don't have enough for more workers to do, you will want to upgrade just to have them move faster.
  One worker can go down the road to remove the next obstruction and build the next bridge before the road is built.
  A free worker needs to build and upgrade all buildings including the camp.
  Don't let the man working at the building fool you; you can't control him.

Buildings:  Every level starts with a level 1 camp (1 worker) and that is all.  Look around to see what buildings are available before you get started.  Every now and then a level will not provide you with a resource building (such as food) but that means there should be enough to pick up on that level.  See below for each building type.

Production times:  Production buildings produce their product every 15 seconds for level 1 buildings.  Production does not restart until you pick up the product.  Each time you upgrade the building the production time increases.  However it is still more efficient to upgrade as each upgrade does get you more product per second of recycle time.  However, most of the walkthrough have you stop upgrading at Level 1 until the storehouse or other more important things are built.

Circles:  When you hover the cursor, you will also see a circle around that area. 
  Green--you are able to perform that action right away.  B
  Blue--you have enough resources, but you need to wait until a worker comes back to camp.
  Yellow--you almost have enough resources
  Red--the area is not within your reach because of obstacles
  White--a worker is on the way to build it

Other signs are always seen whether or not you are hovering or clicking.

Obstacles--These will block your forward progress, both for road building and getting to other area.  There are a few times in the later levels where you will be able to scoot around some obstacles to get further down the road.  The walkthrough mentions the 2 times it is imperative to ignore something so as to finish the level quickly.

  Bridge, wood--5 wood
  Bridge, stone--3 wood, 4 stone

Predators:  Many levels have predators, wolves, bears, lions.  Some levels have them in several locations.  Your worker will not come out to work until you click on it to make it go away.  Your worker will continue to work though.  Interestingly if you see your workers all the sudden start moving faster, it might be because a predator is out and they are running home.  Also a building that had a green circle will suddenly turn red.  They also make their growling sounds to let you know they are there. 

Sound volume:  I usually turn off my sound so I can listen to something else when I am playing.  With this game it is extremely useful to leave your sound on low.  It will bing whenever a building produces.  Your predator will make a growling noise.  A new bonus popping up will be make another unique side.  It is worth listening to the worker building noises and the pig grunting when you pick up food and some wheezy kind of sound on the boat (worker has asthma or squeaking oar locks?!)

Roads of Rome compared to My Kingdom for the Princess:

This game is very, very similar to My Kingdom.  It definitely uses similar programming.  Experts at My Kingdom will find this game somewhat easier but not enough to not play the game.  Some of the differences are:

1. 
  Each upgrade in the camp will not only provide another worker but increase
  their overall speed by a perceptible amount.

 2. Your worker
    must actually go to a building to build or upgrade it.

 3. You must pick
    up the resources as they are produced from a building.

 4. You actually
    expend energy (food) to pick up resources laying around (trees, piles of rock
    and stone

 5. The only
    resource that regenerates are berry bushes (and not on the snow episode 2)

 6. You don't
    have to build the roads off the main road.

 7. Additional
    items:  Forts, balloons, boats.

 8. Crisper
    graphics and more detail.
