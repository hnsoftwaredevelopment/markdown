# Roads of Rome 2 Walkthrough & Cheats   



The Roads of Rome 2 Walkthrough contains   everything you need to know to help Victorius find the magic potion to save   Caesar's life. Every level is covered and lots of images are provided.   

## Hints

Similarity to First Game:  This game plays exactly the same as the previous game with the following exceptions:

1.  Relaxed mode and
    Challenge mode:  You cannot switch from one to the other in the middle of
    the game.  If you get stuck on a level, you will have to start in the
    relaxed mode (with the same player if you want) and work up to that point. 
    If you want, you can play a level first in the relaxed mode and the repeat it
    in the challenge mode.  The relaxed mode has completely removed the timer.

 2.  Bonus
     level.  This is the motivation to be an expert on the challenge
     levels.  While you can progress onto the next level if you finish before
     the day is over, the expert rank is what gamers will want to achieve.

 3.  Ladders and
     ropes.  These cost only 2 food to build.  While you can have more
     than one worker on the other side of a rope, they are a little bit slower to
     cross than a bridge.

 4.  Teleporter.
      You will usually need to find a key and then unlock the teleporter before
     you can use it.  After that there is no waiting to get to the other
     side.  You will just run through it.

 5.  Altars. 
     You will pay the stated offering and the large obstruction will clear. 
     Depending on the level, this will be either a flooded area, fog, or fire.

 6.  Caves with
     tops blown off.  Once you clear the opening to a cave, the top will come
     off and you can see what is waiting for you inside. 

 7.  Submarine
     station.  This works just like a boat except it goes underwater. 

 8.  Smithy. 
     This building that allows your workers to build buildings, roads, and bridges
     faster.

 9.  
  Workhouse.  This building allows your workers to remove obstructions and
  pick up resources faster.

 10.  No mini-games!

General Strategy

1.  A good general build
    strategy is to do the following:

 2.  Upgrade the
     camp to level 2 first thing.

 3.  Build the
     sawmill and farm first if you can get to them. 

 4.  Usually build
     the mine next because you can't clear obstructions without the mine.

 5.  Build the
     quarry.

 6.  Next if you
     have storehouses, you should build those even if you have to work to get to
     them.  These add 1 resource to each load brought in, including berries,
     trees, gold piles, and stone piles.

 7.  Upgrade the
     storehouse if you have an upgrade available.

 8.  Then start
     upgrading your buildings.  Most of the time you don't need to upgrade the
     sawmill. 

 9.  Consider
     upgrading your camp before upgrading other buildings because of the worker plus
     speed benefit. 

 10.  Don't start
      building the road until you know you have enough food to remove the obstacles
      to all of the production buildings including the storehouse.

 11.  New to the second
      game are the smithy and workshop.  These will permanently speed up your
      workers' obstacle clearing and building speed.  While great to have at the
      end, I usually find that I need more resources rather than more speed, so
      upgrade everything else before building these.

In addition: 

1.  Try to keep your
    workers moving as much as possible.

 2.  Always pick
     the berries if your worker is not doing anything else.

 3.  Prioritize
     picking berries on all levels except the snowy ones because they
     regenerate.  Snow levels don't regenerate their berry bushes.

 4.  Pick up
     resources at each building quickly because they don't start producing again
     until the resources are picked up.

 5.  The further
     apart things are, the more you need a higher level camp.  Seriously
     consider upgrading your camp to at least level 3 before upgrading other
     buildings unless you are seriously short of a particular resource.

 6.  Don't feel
     bad about chopping down trees.  We're Romans; we're not
     conservationists.  Seriously you will need a lot of wood to get things
     going quickly.

 7.  Don't feel
     bad about using up your food to chop down trees to get your buildings producing
     quickly.  Just keep an eye on whether you have obstructions blocking your
     buildings that you need the food for.

 8.  If you aren't
     successful the first time, switch things up a bit.  Notice what you were
     running short of and prioritize that the next time.  Seriously consider
     upgrading your warehouse sooner than you might think it needs to be done.

 9.  Don't start
     building your road until you know you have enough food to get through the rocks
     blocking the way to the storehouse. 

 10.  Remember you can
      switch modes to Relaxed at any point in the game if you decide you don't want
      the time pressure.

 11.  Many times you
      will be able to work around an obstacle even when it looks like you might not
      be able to.  So always hover over your destination to see if you can get
      there sooner.

Gameplay Information

Episodes and levels:  There are 4 episodes in the game, each with 10 levels.  Each episode has its own unique terrain and will introduce more and more challenges.  While buildings, bridges, and resources do not cost anymore in later levels, all the obstacles and roads cost increasingly more.  The regenerating berries vary according to terrain.

Timer:  Clicking anywhere on the screen start starts the timer.  You cannot check costs until the timer has started, so just click, check things out, and then restart your level after you have decided your plan of attack.  If you finish before the little line on the left, you will receive an expert rank.

Workers: 

 - With each level you
start with 1 worker.  Each time you upgrade the camp you get an additional
worker and all of them move and work more quickly.  So sometimes even if
you don't have enough for more workers to do, you will want to upgrade just to
have them move faster.

 - A worker can go down
the road to remove the next obstruction and build the next bridge before the
road is built.

 - A free worker needs
to build and upgrade all buildings including the camp.

 - Don't let the man
working at the building fool you; you can't control him.

Buildings:  Every level starts with a level 1 camp (1 worker) and that is all.  Look around to see what buildings are available before you get started.  Every now and then a level will not provide you with a resource building but that means there should be enough to pick up on that level.  See below for each building type.

Production times:  Production buildings produce their product every 15 seconds for level 1 buildings.  Production does not restart until you pick up the product.  Each time you upgrade the building the production time increases.  However it is still more efficient to upgrade as each upgrade does get you more product per second of recycle time.  However, most of the walkthrough has you stop upgrading at Level 1 until the storehouse or other more important things are built.

Circles:  When you hover the cursor, you will also see a circle around that area. 
  Green--you are able to perform that action right away. 
  Blue--you have enough resources, but you need to wait until a worker comes back to camp.
  Yellow--you almost have enough resources
  Red--the area is not within your reach because of obstacles
  White--a worker is on the way to build it

Other signs are always seen whether or not you are hovering or clicking.

Obstacles--These will block your forward progress, both for road building and getting to other area.  There are many times in the later levels where you will be able to scoot around some obstacles to get further down the road.  Especially when you are trying to get to a vital building, always check to see if you can wait to remove an obstacle by going around it. 
  Fallen tree--3 food, 2 gold up to 5 food, 4 gold cost; gives 4 wood (this is the only obstacle that gives you a resource when you remove it)
  Bridge, wood--5 wood
  Bridge, stone--5 wood, 6 stone
  Heaps of stone and snowdrifts--vary in price, but always cost food and gold to clear.  Overall prices increase on each level, up to 9 food and 9 gold at the end of the game.

Swamps, cracks, and flooded--vary in price, gold and food.  Removing theses obstacles will allow you to work further down the road.  You will still need to build the road over it if it is on the road.  There are a few times where these areas will be almost imperceptible.  You will wonder why another obstacle continues to have a red circle around it. 

Goal items:  All of the below cost varying amounts of gold and food.  Each level will cost more for each item.  They are frequently in out of the way spots.  Don't forget to get them.  There's nothing worse than finishing the road and wondering why the level hasn't finished.  Occasionally a crystal will be inside a cave.  Always explore the caves.
