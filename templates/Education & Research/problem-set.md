## Problem 1

Given $f(x) = x^2 + 3x - 5$, find $f'(x)$.

**Solution:**

$$
f'(x) = 2x + 3
$$

## Problem 2

Evaluate the integral:

$$
\\int_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x) \\, dx
$$

**Solution:**

$$
= \\left[ x^3 + x^2 \\right]_0^1 = (1 + 1) - (0) = 2
$$

## Problem 3

Solve the system of equations:

$$
\\begin{cases} 2x + y = 5 \\\\ x - y = 1 \\end{cases}
$$

**Solution:**

Adding the equations: $3x = 6$, so $x = 2$, $y = 1$.
