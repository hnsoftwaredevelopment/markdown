## Opening (5 min)

Set context and ground rules:
- One speaker at a time
- Build on each other's ideas
- Challenge ideas, not people

## Key Questions

### Question 1 (10 min)

> Main question here?

**Follow-ups:**
- Can you give an example?
- How does this connect to [related topic]?

### Question 2 (10 min)

> Another key question?

**Follow-ups:**
- What evidence supports this?
- Does anyone have a different perspective?

### Question 3 (10 min)

> Deeper/harder question?

**Follow-ups:**
- What are the implications?
- How would you apply this?

## Wrap-Up (10 min)

- Key takeaways from the group
- Any unresolved questions?
- Preview of next discussion topic
