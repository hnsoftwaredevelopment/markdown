## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | Frame ROI: "Our customers typically see X% improvement…" |
| "We already have a solution" | "What's missing from your current workflow?" |
| "Need to think about it" | "What specific concerns can I address now?" |
| "Not the right time" | "When would be the right time? Can we schedule a follow-up?" |
