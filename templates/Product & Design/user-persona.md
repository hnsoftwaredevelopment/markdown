# User Persona: My Document

---

## Demographics

- **Name:** Alex Chen (fictional)
- **Age:** 32
- **Role:** Product Manager
- **Location:** San Francisco, CA
- **Tech Comfort:** High

## Goals

1. Ship features faster with less friction
2. Keep stakeholders aligned without endless meetings
3. Make data-driven decisions

## Pain Points

1. Too many tools, information scattered
2. Context switching kills productivity
3. Reporting is manual and time-consuming

## Behaviors

- Checks email first thing, then Slack
- Prefers async communication
- Reads documentation before asking questions
- Values keyboard shortcuts

## Scenario

> Alex is preparing for a quarterly planning session. They need to pull metrics from three different dashboards, synthesize findings, and share with the leadership team by EOD.

## Quotes

> "I just want one place where everything lives."

> "If it takes more than 3 clicks, I'll find a workaround."
