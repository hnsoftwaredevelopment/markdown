## KPI Definitions

| KPI | Formula | Owner | Source | Frequency | Target |
|-----|---------|-------|--------|-----------|--------|
| DAU | Count of unique users per day | Product | Events | Daily | 10K |
| MRR | Sum of active subscription revenue | Finance | Billing | Monthly | $50K |
| Churn Rate | Lost customers / Total customers × 100 | CS | CRM | Monthly | <5% |
| NPS | % Promoters - % Detractors | CS | Survey | Quarterly | >50 |
| CAC | Total Sales+Marketing Spend / New Customers | Marketing | Books | Monthly | <$100 |
| LTV | ARPU × Avg Customer Lifespan | Finance | Combined | Quarterly | >$500 |
| Activation Rate | Users completing onboarding / Signups × 100 | Product | Events | Weekly | >60% |

## Interpretation Guide

- **Leading indicators:** DAU, Activation Rate - predict future performance
- **Lagging indicators:** MRR, Churn - confirm past performance
- **Ratio metrics:** LTV/CAC should be > 3:1 for healthy unit economics
